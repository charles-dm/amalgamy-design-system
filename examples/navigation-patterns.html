[01] Horizontal Tabs top indicator · tinted fill · lowercase · zebra-free

Default for switching between sibling views. Borderless container — tabs sit directly on the page with a 1px floor and 1px vertical dividers between cells. The active tab carries a 2px --color-accent (teal) indicator at the top edge plus a 10% --color-accent-subtle (teal-deep) wash filling the cell. Lowercase labels at body size; the visual quietness is what tells users these aren't buttons. Active state: top indicator + tinted fill + text-primary + medium weight.

[02] Breadcrumb monospace · slash-separated · status-bar resident

Always lives inside the status bar. Lowercase, monospace, slash-separated. Brightness rises left-to-right toward the current location. Three segments is typical, four is the cap — deeper than that, switch to a sidebar or compress with ….

[03] Vertical Sidebar 240px · sectioned · numbered · sticky

Full app-shell pattern. Three-part structure: brand mark + subtitle, sectioned link list, and an active state with three signals (text shifts to --color-accent, 2px left border, surface lifts to --color-surface-2). Hover lifts text and surface but does NOT add the left border — that's reserved for "you are here."

[04] Sub-Nav filter chips · segmented control · subordinate

Filter or toggle within a single section. Two flavors: filter chips for filtering a list, segmented control for mutually-exclusive view modes. Neither uses aqua. Lives below the section header it belongs to, with 16–24px of space above it — never tucked inside sec-head.

[05] Compositions which navs go together

Most prototypes use exactly one of: tabs or breadcrumb-deep or sidebar. Sub-nav layers on top of any of them. Avoid stacking tabs and a sidebar at the same level — they create competing top-level navs.
